## AI Frontier

- **Humanoid robotics:** The frontier narrative remains deployment, not pure demo. Current summaries frame 2026 as the year humanoids moved into factory/warehouse pilots: Tesla Optimus in internal production work, Figure at BMW, Apptronik at Mercedes, Agility Digit in logistics, and 1X in home pilots.
- **World models / spatial AI:** The key debate is whether video-trained world models are shippable products or still expensive demos. The most practical near-term uses look like robotics policy learning, simulation, scene understanding, and gameplay/video generation.
- **Scientific AI:** Anthropic's cryptanalysis work is the morning's strongest scientific-AI example: a frontier model used as a research collaborator for finding mathematical weaknesses, with humans validating the results.
- **Commercial agents:** OpenAI's security CLI is another sign that "agentic AI" is migrating into developer workflow automation: scan, triage, verify, and wire into CI.
