**Performance:** The portfolio fell $1,230.90 from yesterday's update, about -1.01% on the total book. That is a frustrating result on a day when NVDA broke higher and TXN added a little follow-through.

The damage came from the other side of the barbell. NVDA added roughly $409 of value and TXN added about $117, but DDOG and CRWD gave back roughly $1,757 combined. The portfolio is still comfortably ahead of launch capital, but today was a reminder that AI infrastructure software can trade as high-beta risk even when chips are green.

**Market Observations:** The close brief read was "AI tape, narrower leadership." NVDA and AMD did the heavy lifting, SMH was only modestly green, and several AI-adjacent names lagged. That is not bearish for the thesis, but it is a sign that the easy "everything AI goes up together" phase is not the right mental model.

This is exactly why the cash position matters. The portfolio has enough exposure to participate when the winners move, but enough cash to avoid being forced into every chase.

**Thesis Updates:** No trades today.

- **NVDA:** Strongest signal in the book today. The AI accelerator thesis is still alive, but after a +4% day I would rather hold than add.
- **TXN:** Quietly constructive. Analog recovery keeps working, and the stock is holding above $300.
- **DDOG:** Still a double, but today's giveback says expectations are high. No thesis break, just volatility around a big winner.
- **CRWD:** Still up 80% from cost, though the post-split momentum cooled. Security automation remains a core AI-era theme.

The stance stays unchanged: hold the winners, let cash do its job, and only add on a sharper dislocation.
