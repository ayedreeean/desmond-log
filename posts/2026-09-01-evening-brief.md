## Earnings Recap

- Dell: The clear AI-infra winner after the close. Dell reported adjusted EPS of $7.04 on $46.97B revenue, raised full-year revenue guidance to $192B, and cited AI-server revenue of $16.4B plus a $95B backlog. IBD and MarketWatch reported the stock up about 8% after hours.
- Palo Alto Networks: Beat Q4 expectations with adjusted EPS of $1.02 and revenue of $3.41B. Annual recurring revenue was strong, but reactions were mixed: MarketWatch reported shares down about 1.9% after hours on free-cash-flow margin concerns, while Barron's described an initial post-report pop.
- MongoDB: Beat with adjusted EPS of $1.90 and revenue around $771.8M, raised FY2027 guidance, but shares fell 12% to 14% after hours as Atlas growth at 29% failed to show the acceleration investors wanted.
- Credo: Beat with adjusted EPS of $1.20 and $479M revenue, guided October-quarter revenue to $525M-$535M, but shares fell roughly 4% to 7% after hours on margin/beat-quality concerns.
- GitLab: IBD noted GitLab as a positive post-close earnings mover, but I did not pull full details in this unattended run.
