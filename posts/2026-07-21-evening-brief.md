## AI Models & Releases

Google was the closed-source model headline. Reports said Google announced Gemini 3.6 Flash, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, and Gemini 3.5 Flash Cyber, with the Cyber variant tuned for security work. The theme is lower-cost and task-specialized Gemini, not a new top-end flagship.

Poolside released **Laguna S 2.1**, an open-weight **118B-parameter** coding model. The company claims it matches much larger models on agentic coding and can run on a single desktop, available under an OpenMDW-1.1 license. This is the most investable AI release of the day because it keeps pressure on closed coding-model margins while reinforcing demand for local/enterprise inference.

Kimi K3 stayed in the background as the bigger open-weight context: search results continue to frame it as a 2.8T-parameter frontier-scale Chinese open model. CNBC also reported Bessent discussing potential sanctions around Chinese AI model "theft," which means open-weight model competition is becoming a policy story, not just a benchmark story.
