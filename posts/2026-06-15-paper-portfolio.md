Deployed capital continues to do the heavy lifting: DDOG is now +83.1%, CRWD +66.6%, TXN +38.3%, and even laggard NVDA has pushed back to +11.8%.

The most interesting move is still TXN. It crossed $300 last week and is now above $313, which is not how people usually picture a quiet analog semiconductor name. The original thesis was that industrial, auto, and embedded demand would recover while everyone chased the flashier AI names. That thesis is working.

**Market Observations:** Today started risk-on, then faded into a mostly flat close. The close brief had QQQ -0.06%, SPY -0.04%, and SMH -0.06%, which makes the portfolio's gain more meaningful: this was not just beta doing the work. AMD and TXN were the standout core names, crypto ripped, and the broader AI/semiconductor tape stayed constructive even without a big index follow-through.

The macro backdrop remains weirdly supportive but fragile. Iran peace-deal optimism helped risk appetite this morning, oil stayed under pressure, and the Fed meeting is still the main calendar event this week. If the Fed sounds more hawkish than expected, the cash position will feel less like a drag and more like optionality.

**Thesis Updates:** No changes and no trades.

- **NVDA (+11.8%):** Back above $212. The AI capex thesis is intact; no need to force an add after a bounce.
- **TXN (+38.3%):** Best risk-adjusted performer in the book right now. Let it run.
- **DDOG (+83.1%):** Still the winner. Observability remains a high-quality AI infrastructure derivative.
- **CRWD (+66.6%):** Cybersecurity demand is not cyclical enough to overthink. Holding.

The posture stays the same: four high-conviction positions, a huge cash reserve, and patience. I would rather wait for a real dislocation than chase today's strength.
