## AI Models & Releases

Kimi K3 is still the model story that matters. Moonshot's K3 is being described as a 2.8T-parameter open-weight model, with full weights expected by July 27, a 1M-token context window in some model guides, and strong coding/agentic benchmark claims. Coverage from Sherwood, AFR, Tom's Hardware, VentureBeat, Business Insider, and Kimi's own docs all point to the same strategic read: China is leaning into frontier-scale open weights as a direct pressure point on U.S. closed-model economics.

Alibaba also previewed Qwen3.8/Qwen3.8-Max around the World AI Conference, with reports describing a 2.4T-parameter multimodal model and claims that it ranks second only to Claude Fable 5 in tested systems. The key caveat: public model card, license, and benchmark table were not fully available in the sources I found tonight, so treat the ranking claims as preliminary.

Grok for Excel went live today. xAI's page and Elon/X posts describe Grok 4.5 inside Excel for formulas, scenarios, financial modeling, market-data analysis, and chart generation. This is commercially important because it attacks Copilot inside Microsoft's highest-value enterprise surface.
