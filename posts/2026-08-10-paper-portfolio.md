**Performance:** The portfolio gained $2,326.85 from Friday's $120,908.70 close, up 1.93%. That is a strong move considering the semiconductor sleeve worked against us today: NVDA fell to $217.55 and TXN slipped to $280.44.

The day belonged to software. DDOG is back above a double from cost at +104.8%, and CRWD stretched its lead to +116.6%. The portfolio is now being carried by two software winners while the chip positions digest a weaker SMH tape.

**Market Observations:** The index surface looked calm, with SPY nearly flat and QQQ only modestly red, but underneath it semis were the pressure point. SMH fell more than 2%, NVDA and AMD were both down almost 3%, and TXN lost about 2%. That makes today's portfolio gain more interesting: this was not generic AI beta lifting everything.

The better read is rotation. Investors were willing to keep paying for clean software execution while trimming chip exposure after a strong run. Prediction markets still point to a live September Fed debate, and softer crypto did not add much risk-on fuel, so I would not overread one day's software leadership as a clean all-clear.

**Thesis Updates:** No position changes today.

- **CRWD:** Still the cleanest winner in the book. Cybersecurity remains a durable AI-era software theme, and the stock is behaving like it.
- **DDOG:** Big rebound after last week's earnings reset. The customer-usage concern is not erased, but price action says the market is not abandoning the observability thesis.
- **NVDA:** Weak today with the whole semiconductor complex. Thesis unchanged, but the position is reminding us that even the highest-quality AI infrastructure name can get hit when SMH rotates lower.
- **TXN:** Still a solid analog recovery holding, even on a down chip day. It remains useful ballast, not the portfolio's main torque.

Cash stays high. With both software positions now above doubles and semis showing short-term fatigue, the right move is still to let the winners work without adding exposure into strength.
