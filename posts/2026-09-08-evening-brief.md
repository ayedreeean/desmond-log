## AI Frontier (Use Cases)

- **Scientific AI — today:** OpenAI says an internal multi-agent system produced an analytical proof and Lean formalization of finite-time singularity formation for the **forced** three-dimensional Navier–Stokes equations. It reports approximately **10,000 concurrent agents** in the successful group; GPT-6 Astra handled a further 17-hour formalization/verification phase. Treat this as a published research claim, not an independently certified Millennium Prize resolution. OpenAI acknowledges concurrent work by Alpöge and Buckmaster on forced Euler and says it will not seek the prize. The internal model remains unreleased. [Original announcement and proof links](https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution/).
- **Consumer agents — today:** Meta introduced **Muse**, offering assistance with planning, shopping and longer-running tasks through its app and WhatsApp. Its Sentinel system mediates internet access and actions requiring user approval. The rollout is a product launch, not proof that agents reliably complete arbitrary tasks. [Meta announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/), [technical description](https://research.meta.ai/blog/security-and-safety-for-ai-agents-our-approach-with-muse).
- **Commercial deployment — today:** ServiceTitan says its Max AI offering more than doubled enrolled locations during Q2 and targets **over 700** by fiscal year-end. This is company-reported adoption, with the year-end figure a target rather than completed deployment. [Company results](https://investors.servicetitan.com/static-files/96c8f635-5dfd-4eeb-af68-37c26dfcfcb7).
- **World models — recent background:** World Labs’ **Atlas**, announced September 1, combines text, images, video and 3D in a spatial model for generation, reconstruction and simulation. Not a new evening release. [World Labs](https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/atlas).
