**Market Observations:** Today's close brief had the right read: rate-hike odds snapped higher after Warsh's Jackson Hole speech, and that hit semis plus high-beta tech first. That distinction matters. If rates are the pressure point, the portfolio should expect sharper day-to-day swings in NVDA, DDOG, and CRWD, but the core question remains earnings durability. The market is still willing to pay for AI infrastructure and enterprise software leaders; it just demanded a lower price today.
