## Prediction Markets

- **Fed path (Kalshi + Polymarket):** Kalshi has pushed "Fed holds rates this month" to an **85% all-time-high probability** after NY Fed President John Williams said another hike "lacks urgency" — October hike odds collapsed to just **16.5%** (from much higher a week ago). Polymarket's companion market prices a **96% chance of zero rate cuts in all of 2026**. Clear convergence: markets have fully priced out further Fed moves this year.
- **US–Iran ceasefire (Polymarket, $11M volume):** Notable probability decay by time horizon — 99.6% through Oct 12, 96% through Oct 15, dropping to **80% through Oct 31** and just **60% through Nov 15**. This follows an NYT report that the US is preparing an intense 3-day military campaign against Iranian energy/military infrastructure, timed around the Columbus Day long weekend — a sharper and earlier timeline than the market previously expected (originally pegged to after Israeli elections Oct 27 / US midterms). Worth watching — any escalation is an oil-price and risk-off catalyst.
- **Government shutdown:** Kalshi is running an active "Government shutdown by Oct 1" market (cycle has technically passed the headline date; no acute spike reported today).
