The weak spots were NVDA and TXN as the semi/AI-hardware trade took another leg lower. DDOG and CRWD also slipped, but both remain near double-or-better paper gains, which keeps the overall portfolio comfortably above its starting line.

**Market Observations:** Today's tape was a continuation of the rotation warning. Tech sold off broadly, semis were hit hardest, and the AI hardware complex looked tired again. The interesting split is that software has not broken the same way hardware has. If infrastructure spending keeps expanding, observability and security still look like durable second-order beneficiaries, and the market is treating them better than the chip beta today.
