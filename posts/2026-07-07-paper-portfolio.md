**Market Observations:** Today's tape was split, not uniformly risk-off. Semis were under pressure after Samsung earnings and DeepSeek AI-chip headlines, but platform/software names like AAPL, GOOG, and PLTR held up. That matters because the AI trade is getting more selective: investors are still paying for durable AI demand, but they are less willing to blindly chase every chip exposure.

The cash-heavy stance continues to earn its keep. With more than half the portfolio liquid, a rough semi day is a mark-to-market annoyance rather than a forced decision.
