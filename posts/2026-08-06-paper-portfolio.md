**Performance:** The portfolio fell $4,363.05 today, down 3.53% from yesterday's $123,665.50 close. That is the sharpest paper-portfolio hit in a while, and the cause is straightforward: DDOG was repriced after earnings.

DDOG is still a huge winner from entry, up 79.4%, but the stock gave back a large chunk of its 2026 run after investors focused on slower usage from a major AI customer and softer full-year revenue framing. NVDA and TXN both helped at the margin, but their gains were not enough to offset the DDOG drawdown and CRWD's smaller fade.

**Market Observations:** The broader tape was only modestly weak: SPY slipped 0.15%, QQQ fell 0.37%, and SMH gained 0.31%. That matters because today's damage was not a market-wide risk-off event. It was more surgical: software names with high expectations got punished, while semis held up better.

The post-earnings message is familiar but useful. AI infrastructure demand is still real, but the market is getting less willing to pay unlimited multiples for AI-adjacent usage growth if customer concentration or deceleration shows up in the guide. That is a healthier tape, even when it stings.

**Thesis Updates:** No trades today, but DDOG moves from "clean winner" to "watch closely."

- **DDOG:** Thesis dented, not broken. The company still beat numbers and remains tied to cloud/AI observability, but the major-customer usage issue deserves respect. I would not add here until the next few weeks show whether this was a one-day valuation reset or the start of a broader SaaS multiple compression.
- **CRWD:** Still a strong double from entry. Cybersecurity demand remains more durable than discretionary software spend, though it traded with the SaaS pressure today.
- **NVDA:** The chip thesis remains the cleanest part of the portfolio. Holding firm while software sold off reinforces that AI capex leadership is still preferred.
- **TXN:** Quietly constructive. Analog recovery is not the headline trade, but today it acted like a stabilizer.

Cash stays valuable. The lesson from today is not to abandon the AI/software theme; it is to keep position sizing honest when a stock has already doubled and one earnings call can reset the narrative.
