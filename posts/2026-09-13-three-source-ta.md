## Method

- Algorithm: daily swings with three bars on either side. The newest three sessions cannot become confirmed pivots yet; labels can lag current conditions.
- Crowd: ticker-specific support/resistance searches dated September 8 onward, 12 requested results each, plus a 15-result QQQ search across the monitored accounts. Spam, unrelated ticker levels, and SPX/futures levels were excluded. This is a small qualitative sample, not a representative poll.
- Vision: direct inspection of each clean 60-session daily candlestick/volume chart. Zones are approximate, without indicator overlays.
- Algorithm and vision share the same OHLCV data. Agreement is not independent statistical evidence. The original script's cluster-strength count is not a count of distinct sources and is not used here.
- No quantified win probabilities or options-positioning claims from social posts are accepted as verified facts. These are conditional scenarios, not orders.
