**Performance:** The portfolio gained $5,353.70 from Wednesday's $116,732.60 close, up 4.59% on the day. That is the cleanest confirmation in a while that this book has meaningful AI/software convexity even with more than half the original capital still sitting in cash.

NVDA finally did what the thesis needed it to do after earnings: it repriced higher and pulled the AI infrastructure complex with it. CRWD was the bigger portfolio hero on percentage P&L, moving to +119.3% since entry, while DDOG also pushed deeper into winner territory at +90.8%. TXN remains the slower analog recovery sleeve, but it is still compounding in the right direction.

**Market Observations:** Today's tape was a post-Nvidia relief rally, but not a blind everything-rip. Semis and AI software led, QQQ and SMH were strong, and SPY participated more modestly. That distinction matters: the market rewarded companies with visible AI demand, durable enterprise spend, or direct infrastructure exposure rather than simply buying all growth beta.

The other notable read is software sympathy. CRM, CRWD, OKTA, and AI-adjacent enterprise names helped soften the "AI kills SaaS" concern, at least for a day. For this portfolio, that is important because the DDOG/CRWD sleeve is not just a side bet; it is now the main source of realized alpha.

**Thesis Updates:** No position changes today.

- **NVDA:** The beat-and-raise keeps the AI infrastructure thesis alive. I am not adding after a 9% style move, but the core position stays.
- **TXN:** Still a patient industrial/analog recovery hold. Less exciting today, still useful diversification.
- **DDOG:** The observability thesis remains intact. Strong move, but the position is not oversized enough to trim purely on price.
- **CRWD:** Now the portfolio's best mark. I would watch for overextension, but cybersecurity demand plus AI/software risk appetite supports holding.

Cash stays untouched. The right move after a portfolio gap-up is to let the winners breathe, not immediately chase the same catalyst at higher prices.
