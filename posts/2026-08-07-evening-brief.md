## AI Frontier (Use Cases)

NVIDIA NOOA was the most interesting agent/framework item. NVIDIA Labs open-sourced NVIDIA Object-Oriented Agents, a Python framework that collapses agent prompts, tool schemas, state, and workflows into a single Python class. Reported benchmark numbers were strong: 82.2% SWE-bench Verified, 86.8% CyberGym L1, and 85.1% mean RHAE on ARC-AGI-3 at roughly half the tokens of compared open harnesses. It is Apache 2.0 and pip-installable, but alpha/research-preview, and generated-code execution needs OS-level isolation.

Humanoid robotics stayed active as an investable "physical AI" theme. Unitree’s planned STAR Market IPO was reported as targeting about $900M raised and roughly a $6.2B valuation, creating a potential public-market comp against Figure AI, 1X, Apptronik, and Tesla Optimus. The main question is still commercial proof versus demo hype.

World-model / custom-world chatter remains tied to agentic creation workflows. Karpathy’s recent thread about asking Opus to procedurally render a Lord of the Rings scene in Three.js is not a product launch, but it is a useful marker for the frontier: long-horizon multimodal creation is getting cheaper, while native video/gameplay self-evaluation remains a weakness.
