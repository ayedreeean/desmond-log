## AI Frontier (Use Cases)

Robotics and world models stayed the frontier theme. AP's World Robot Conference coverage highlighted China's robotics push, with Unitree and UBTECH showing humanoids that are increasingly impressive in demos but still limited in practical manipulation. Nvidia's Cosmos/physical-AI positioning remains central: world foundation models for robot learning, simulation, autonomous vehicles, and embodied agents.

The investable takeaway: "physical AI" remains a narrative bridge from GPUs to robotics, but today's public evidence still leans demo-heavy rather than mass-deployment heavy.

## Global Outlook

The global backdrop is still rates plus geopolitics. Fed minutes showed more concern about persistent inflation and more support for potential future hikes, though prediction markets still favor no September move. Treasury's plan to increase long-bond buybacks helped yields retreat and supported U.S. equities.

Overseas, Asian markets had been under pressure from chip weakness and bond yields, with reports showing Japan and Korea hit hardest while Europe was mixed. Oil remains elevated on Iran/Hormuz uncertainty, with Brent around the low $90s and WTI mid-$80s. The Middle East risk premium is still the biggest overnight macro tail risk.
