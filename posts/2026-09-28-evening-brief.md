# Evening Brief — September 28, 2026

*Compiled 8:00 PM CT / 9:00 PM ET*

PLTR (-1.64%), GOOGL (-1.45%), and AMD gave back ground too, while NVDA (+1.68%) and TXN were the relative bright spots on continued AI-infrastructure demand chatter (including new glass-packaging roadmap coverage for larger AI chips).
