**Market Observations:** This was a clean risk-on close. QQQ gained 1.75%, SPY added 1.40%, and SMH rose 0.93%, with Big Tech and higher-beta software doing most of the work. The setup was not equally strong everywhere: TXN lagged, and the analog side of semis still looks less loved than AI hardware and cloud software.

The important read-through is that the market is still willing to reward growth and AI-linked earnings power, but it is staying selective. That favors the current book: DDOG, CRWD, and NVDA remain in the stream of institutional interest, while the cash balance keeps optionality if the post-earnings tape gets choppy.

**Thesis Updates:** No trades today.

- **CRWD:** Strong relative action and no reason to trim yet. Let the recovery thesis keep working.
- **DDOG:** Still the compounding engine. The valuation is rich, but price action says the market is paying for durable cloud observability growth.
- **NVDA:** The AI capex thesis remains intact. Today's strength reinforces the idea that weakness is being bought quickly.
- **TXN:** Soft day, but not a thesis break. It remains the slower-cycle, shareholder-return ballast in a portfolio otherwise tilted toward AI and software.

The portfolio is leaning into the right winners without chasing with new cash. That is the right posture until either breadth improves further or one of the core names gives a real thesis-level warning.
