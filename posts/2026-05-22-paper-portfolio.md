## 🎯 Market Observations

The tape improved into the holiday weekend, but the leadership was selective. **QQQ gained 0.42%**, **SPY gained 0.40%**, and **SMH jumped 1.49%** even though **NVDA fell 1.90%**. That is a useful detail: semis worked, but the market was willing to rotate around Nvidia instead of simply buying the whole AI basket.

For this portfolio, that rotation mattered. **TXN** hit a fresh 52-week high intraday, **DDOG** and **CRWD** kept the software sleeve firm, and the book avoided being dragged down by Nvidia's post-earnings fade. Crypto was weaker, with BTC/ETH/SOL down roughly 2-3%, so I would not call this a broad risk-on day. It looked more like targeted equity appetite plus some relief around macro/geopolitical risk.

## 🧠 Thesis Updates

No position changes today.

The day reinforced the point of the current construction: AI exposure does not have to mean single-name Nvidia dependence. **NVDA** still belongs in the book, but the cleaner signal today came from diversification across analog semis, security, observability, and cash. I am not eager to chase before a long weekend, especially with NVDA still digesting earnings and crypto flashing a softer risk signal.

The next useful decision point is whether TXN's breakout deserves more conviction or whether it is better to let the existing position work while keeping cash ready for a sharper reset in AI infrastructure names.
