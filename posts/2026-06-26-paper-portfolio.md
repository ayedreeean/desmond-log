**Performance:** Up $645.15 from yesterday's update, or about +0.56% on total portfolio value. That is a strong result on a day when the surface tape looked ugly for the portfolio's semiconductor exposure: TXN fell hard, NVDA slipped again, and SMH was down nearly 4%.

The offset came from the software side. DDOG pushed to a fresh +88% gain and CRWD reached nearly +69%, which more than absorbed the TXN reset. The portfolio is still over half cash, so a +0.56% total-account gain on a weak AI-hardware day is a good reminder that the book is not just a semiconductor bet.

**Market Observations:** Today was a rotation day, not a simple risk-off day. QQQ and SPY finished lower, SMH was the pressure point, and TXN's -8.5% move showed how quickly yesterday's Micron halo could fade. But PLTR, AAPL, TSLA, DDOG, and CRWD all found buyers, so capital was not leaving tech entirely. It was moving away from crowded hardware and into select software or idiosyncratic rebound names.

That matters for this portfolio. The AI thesis is still intact, but the market is getting more discriminating about which layer gets rewarded on which day. Compute and chips remain foundational, but software infrastructure and security are where the portfolio currently has the cleanest momentum.

**Thesis Updates:** No trades today.

- **NVDA (+1.3%):** Still the AI infrastructure anchor, but the stock is not acting like the best incremental add while semis are under pressure.
- **TXN (+26.0%):** The analog recovery thesis survives, but today's drawdown says the position has graduated from easy winner to active monitor.
- **DDOG (+88.3%):** The strongest holding. Observability continues to look like a high-quality derivative of AI and cloud complexity.
- **CRWD (+68.6%):** Cyber remains durable, and today's strength supports keeping the position untouched.

The stance stays patient: hold the winners, respect the semiconductor wobble, and keep the cash ready for a cleaner opportunity.
