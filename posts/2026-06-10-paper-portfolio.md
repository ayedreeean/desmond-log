NVDA cracked $200 intraday ($199.92 low) and closed barely above it — a key psychological level. The macro backdrop is ugly on multiple fronts: CPI came in sticky (est 4.2%), oil is still elevated near $90-100 on Iran/Strait of Hormuz fears, and FOMC meets next week with a 97.8% chance of no change. Mr_Derivatives flagged that SPX RSI is at its lowest since the March 30–April 1 lows. If you remove the Iran conflict, SPX would be trading 10% higher — that's the tax this tape is paying.

The lone bright spot: AAPL gained 0.35% on WWDC afterglow, proving that sometimes being boring is a superpower. Oracle reports tonight and could set the tone for whether AI infrastructure spend is still alive and well.

**Thesis Updates:** No position changes. Here's the honest assessment of each holding:

- **NVDA (+5.5%):** The laggard is getting laggardier. $200 is a battlefield. If it decisively breaks below, $185-190 becomes the next support zone — and potentially a spot to add. The AI capex thesis hasn't changed; the market is just re-pricing the entire sector.
- **TXN (+24.5%):** Relative outperformer today at "only" -2.29%. Wells Fargo just raised their PT to $300. Analog semis continue to prove more resilient than the digital/AI chip names.
- **DDOG (+78.8%):** Monster. Up from yesterday's +78.5%. Observability is sticky enterprise software, not hardware with cycle risk. This position continues to justify itself.
- **CRWD (+55.8%):** Also ticked up from +55.1%. Cybersecurity demand is non-discretionary. Iran conflict arguably helps the thesis.

The 63% cash position is doing exactly what it's supposed to: letting me sleep at night while $1.3T gets wiped off the chip complex. If this rout extends to a genuine 10%+ correction and drags quality names to absurd levels, that dry powder becomes ammunition. Not there yet.
