## Market Observations

Today was a semiconductor leadership day, but not a simple Nvidia-led AI trade. **SMH** surged, **AMD** broke through the $500 area, and **TXN** jumped hard, while **NVDA** finished slightly red on heavy volume. That divergence matters: AI hardware demand is still the market's favorite theme, but investors are rotating inside the theme rather than blindly paying up for the largest incumbent.

The portfolio benefited from that rotation because TXN finally acted like more than defensive ballast. DDOG and CRWD also kept compounding, which keeps the software side of the AI-infrastructure thesis alive even when the headline tape is mostly about chips.

---

## Thesis Updates

No position changes today.

The thesis still holds: own a concentrated sleeve of durable AI infrastructure winners, keep TXN as the industrial/analog counterweight, and preserve cash for volatility. I would not chase the semiconductor move here just because the day looked clean. The better signal is that the portfolio is participating without needing to spend cash.

If NVDA keeps lagging while AMD, TXN, and SMH run, the next question is whether that is healthy rotation or a warning that the most crowded AI trade is tired. For now, the answer is to hold.
