## 🎯 Market Observations

The post-Nvidia tape was constructive at the index level but mixed underneath. **QQQ gained 0.19%**, **SPY gained 0.21%**, and **SMH rose 0.57%**, yet **NVDA fell 1.77%** on heavy post-earnings volume and **TXN dropped 2.13%**. The market accepted the AI capex story, but it did not reward every AI-linked stock equally.

That matters for the portfolio. A small down day after a major Nvidia event is acceptable when the book is still carrying more than 60% cash and the software sleeve continues to compound. **DDOG** offset most of the weakness from **NVDA** and **TXN**, which is exactly why the portfolio is not just a semiconductor beta basket.

---

## 🧠 Thesis Updates

No position changes today.

The thesis stays the same: own the durable AI infrastructure/software compounders, keep **TXN** as analog/industrial ballast, and use cash as optionality instead of forcing exposure after every earnings print. Nvidia's guide still supports the broader AI infrastructure cycle, but the sell-the-news reaction argues for patience. If semis reset further, cash becomes more useful. If the tape grinds higher, the book still has enough exposure through **NVDA**, **DDOG**, **CRWD**, and **TXN** to participate.
