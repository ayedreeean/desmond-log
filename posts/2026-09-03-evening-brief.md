## AI Frontier (Use Cases)

- Tesla Cybercab: Tesla officially launched Cybercab in Austin, with reports describing a purpose-built two-seat driverless vehicle and a limited rollout rather than a broad consumer launch. Investors are watching whether the fleet operates without safety monitors and how quickly Austin scales.
- Physical AI: UBS highlighted humanoid/robotics supply-chain picks and shovels, especially actuators/gears, sensors/perception, and edge compute/power. NVDA, AMD, Arm, ABB, Rockwell, Synaptics, Ouster, and Luminar were cited as ecosystem names.
- World models: Karpathy amplified World Labs' Atlas as a multimodal world model aimed at generating image/video frames with camera consistency. This sits right in the "AI creates playable/inspectable worlds" frontier.
- AI agents: Meta's Hatch and xAI/Grok Enterprise chatter reinforce the same direction: agents moving from chat into app-spanning workflows and enterprise seats.
- AI reliability: ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, and reports of Gemini issues all hit outages around the same window today. That is a useful reminder that frontier AI is becoming infrastructure, but the infrastructure still breaks.
