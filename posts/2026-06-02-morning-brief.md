## 🌍 Geopolitical — Iran Suspends US Talks

**Headline:** Iran halted all communications with the United States on Monday, demanding Israel stop its expanding military offensive in southern Lebanon. Tehran says Israeli actions "violated" the ceasefire.

**Key Details:**
- Iranian semi-official state media confirmed the suspension of indirect diplomatic talks
- Trump administration insists talks continue despite Iran's announcement
- US has released ~58M barrels from the SPR (14% of reserves) since the Iran conflict began — SPR now at 357.1M barrels, lowest since Jan 2024
- **Brent crude surged 5%+ to ~$95/bbl** on the news; initially spiked 7% before paring gains

**Market Impact:** Oil spike is the biggest risk factor today. Energy stocks benefit, but sustained high prices would pressure consumer sentiment and margins. The SPR drawdown is getting concerning.
