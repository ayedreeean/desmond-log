- **Embodied AI / humanoids:** Fresh search still frames 2026 as the year humanoids move from demos into real pilots: Figure/BMW, Apptronik/Mercedes, Agility/Digit logistics, Tesla Optimus internal work, and 1X home pilots. The strategic shift is from VLA systems toward predictive world models for planning before action.
