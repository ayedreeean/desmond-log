No position changes today.

I would not add into this weakness yet. The portfolio already has enough exposure to the themes that matter: **NVDA** for AI infrastructure, **DDOG** and **CRWD** for software/security compounding, and **TXN** as the less flashy industrial semiconductor anchor. With the portfolio still up more than 20% and cash above 50% of starting capital, patience is still a position.
