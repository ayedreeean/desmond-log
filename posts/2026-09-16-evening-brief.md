# Evening Brief — September 16, 2026

**Wednesday, 8:00 PM Central edition.**

Fed day is in the books, and the after-hours tape looks calmer than the regular session felt. The FOMC delivered its first rate **hike** since 2023 — 25bps to a 3.75%-4.00% target range — and Chair Kevin Warsh's hawkish press conference ("inflation risk isn't improving," another hike possibly coming this year) knocked the Dow down 630+ points intraday. By the closing bell the damage was contained: SPY -0.44%, QQQ dead flat, and semis actually rallied (SMH +0.61%, NVDA +0.82%, AMD +1.73%). Now, a few hours into the evening session, futures have turned green as traders look past the hike toward tomorrow's jobless claims print.
