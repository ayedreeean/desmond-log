## AI Models & Releases

**Confirmed fresh open-weight release: YuE2-3B.** Its Hugging Face repository was created September 9 at **18:30 UTC**, inside this edition's 24-hour window. The nominal 3B model's published safetensors metadata totals approximately **3.63 billion parameters**. It generates music using an editable melody/chord plan and supports covers and iterative composition changes. Weights are **CC BY-NC 4.0**, not unrestricted commercial open source. [Model card](https://huggingface.co/m-a-p/YuE2-3B), [repository metadata](https://huggingface.co/api/models/m-a-p/YuE2-3B).

**Author-reported benchmarks:** On 192 WildSongBench prompts, SongBench average is **6.7316** for standard YuE2 and **6.9632** with best-of-eight selection, versus **6.8721** for Suno v5. Candidate selection differs; small mean differences do not establish statistical significance. Code is Apache 2.0. [Primary repository and evaluation protocol](https://github.com/multimodal-art-projection/YuE).
