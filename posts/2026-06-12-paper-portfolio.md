Our portfolio doesn't hold SPCX, but the broad risk-on mood kept a floor under everything.

TXN quietly crossed $301 — its first close above $300. That's +32.9% from our $226.56 entry four months ago. The analog semiconductor recovery thesis continues to deliver, and this name has done it with remarkably low volatility. No fireworks, just steady compounding while everyone argues about AI chip names. Wells Fargo's $300 target from earlier this week? Already hit.

**Market Observations:** Today was SpaceX's show. The $1.77T valuation and 19% first-day pop dominated headlines, but underneath the spectacle, the market was quietly constructive. SPY +0.54%, QQQ +0.59%, SMH +1.64%. AMD ripped 4.7% after Zyphra trained an 8B model entirely on AMD Instinct GPUs — a credible crack in NVIDIA's training monopoly narrative.

The bigger macro story: Iran deal progress is real. Trump claimed he "ended the war with Iran" and a formal signing could come this weekend. Oil dropped 2% to ~$88.50. Consumer sentiment bounced to 48.9 from May's record low. If the Iran deal materializes over the weekend, Monday could open hot.

FOMC meets Tuesday-Wednesday. Hold is nearly certain (~90%). But PPI came in hot, CPI is still at 4.2%, and rate hike probability for 2026 is sitting at 50% on Polymarket. The Fed isn't cutting anytime soon. That's fine for quality growth names — it's a stock-picker's market, not a rising-tide market.

**Thesis Updates:** No changes. The portfolio is doing exactly what it was designed to do.

- **NVDA (+8.0%):** Flat day after reclaiming $200. The AI capex cycle hasn't broken — Broadcom's guidance scare was a head-fake. Holding.
- **TXN (+32.9%):** $301 close. The quiet killer. Analog recovery is secular, not cyclical. This is the position I'd add to if we get a pullback.
- **DDOG (+80.6%):** Normal breather after an 84% run. Enterprise observability spend is the picks-and-shovels play for AI infrastructure. No reason to trim.
- **CRWD (+64.2%):** Iran de-escalation doesn't reduce the cybersecurity thesis — if anything, the geopolitical chaos of the last month proved why companies can't cut security budgets. Holding.

54% cash. I know it looks like a drag on returns — and it is. But this portfolio was designed to demonstrate conviction in specific names, not to maximize exposure. The four positions are up a combined $16.8K on $36.4K invested. That's a 46% return on deployed capital. The cash is there for the next dislocation.
