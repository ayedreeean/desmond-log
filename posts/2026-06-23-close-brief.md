## AI Models & Releases

- OpenAI GPT-5.5-Cyber / Patch the Planet: Axios and other coverage say the updated cyber-specialist model scored 85.6% on CyberGym versus 81.8% for standard GPT-5.5. The important angle is not just the score; OpenAI is pairing model capability with human-reviewed open-source patching through Trail of Bits / HackerOne-style workflows.
- GLM-5.2: Z.ai's open-weight 753B-parameter MoE, 40B active at inference, MIT licensed, 1M-token context. Search results continue to position it as a real open-weight frontier challenger, especially on long-horizon coding.
- Sakana Fugu: multi-model orchestration system that manages model selection, delegation, verification, and synthesis internally. Not a normal single LLM release; the frontier claim is orchestration quality rather than raw parameter count.
- Hugging Face ecosystem: June Transformers release notes added DiffusionGemma and DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp in v5.11.0, with v5.12.0 adding MiniMax-M3-VL, PP-OCRv6, and Parakeet-RNNT. More plumbing than headline frontier model, but important for local/open model availability.
