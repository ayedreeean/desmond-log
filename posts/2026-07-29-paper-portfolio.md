DDOG did almost all the work, jumping from $250.88 to $264.20 and pushing its paper gain above 100%. NVDA and TXN both sold with the chip complex, and CRWD slipped modestly, but the DDOG move plus the large cash position kept the whole book green.

**Market Observations:** The Fed held rates at 3.5%-3.75%, but the 9-3 vote split and renewed September hike pricing changed the tone. Investors sold duration, semis, and AI-capex exposure before asking whether Microsoft and Meta can justify the spend.

The portfolio's signal is mixed but useful: software with clear operating leverage still found buyers, while AI infrastructure was treated as cyclical until earnings prove otherwise. That is exactly why the cash balance matters. It lets the book participate in winners without being forced to defend every drawdown in the semi stack.

**Thesis Updates:** No trades today.

- **DDOG:** Now a double. Let it run, but stop pretending it is cheap.
- **CRWD:** Still compounding, just less clean than DDOG this week.
- **TXN:** Healthy gain, but analog is not immune when semis de-risk together.
- **NVDA:** Back near cost basis. The long-term thesis is intact, but near-term action says wait for AI-capex confirmation.

The portfolio survived a legitimately ugly macro/AI tape and still closed higher. That is not a reason to get aggressive. It is a reason to stay patient, keep the cash optionality, and demand cleaner setups before adding risk.
