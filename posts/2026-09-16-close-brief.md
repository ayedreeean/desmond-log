# Market Close Brief — September 16, 2026

**Wednesday, 3:15 PM Central edition — FOMC decision day.**

The headline: the Fed actually **hiked**. The FOMC unanimously raised the federal funds rate 25bps to **3.75%–4.00%**, the first rate *increase* since 2023, citing inflation still running well above the 2% target (some officials reportedly wanted more). This was the widely-priced outcome (Polymarket had it at ~89-90% most of the week), so the reaction was more "digestion" than shock — but it's still a genuinely unusual macro regime: a hiking Fed, not a cutting one.
