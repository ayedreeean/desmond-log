## AI Frontier (Use Cases)

- **Humanoid robotics:** Hyundai's CEO said car dealerships could eventually sell humanoid or quadruped robots, tied to Boston Dynamics, Atlas mass-production planning, and Hyundai Capital exploring robot financing. That's a commercialization signal: robotics is being packaged like a durable-goods market, not just a lab demo.
- **Tesla Optimus / Unitree context:** Recent reporting still frames Tesla Optimus production as delayed into the coming months with commercial sales more likely in 2H 2027, while Unitree's CEO remains cautious that general humanoid autonomy may take years.
- **World models:** The current frontier theme is "physical AI": world models trained on simulation, video, and game data to help robots understand 3D environments and plan actions. WSJ highlighted General Intuition and Emulate as examples; 1X's earlier 1XWM work remains relevant for video-to-action robot policies.
- **Enterprise agents:** Same-day enterprise AI-agent coverage focused on orchestration, governance, memory, tool integration, human approval, and production controls. The practical deployment story is less "agent demo" and more "who can safely connect agents to real systems."
- **Scientific AI:** Recent science coverage pointed to AI identifying a DNA initiation signature in roughly 60% of human genes after analyzing about 500,000 sequences, another example of AI moving into instrument/discovery workflows.
