- **Tesla:** Revenue beat at about $28.24B-$28.26B, but adjusted EPS of $0.33 badly missed Street expectations near $0.50-$0.54. Operating expenses rose sharply, operating margin fell to about 1.4%, and free cash flow was negative. Shares fell roughly 3%-4% after hours.
- **Alphabet:** Revenue beat at $119.8B versus roughly $116.9B expected, but the stock fell more than 4% after the company lifted 2026 capex guidance to roughly $195B-$205B. The market liked demand, disliked the spend.
- **Texas Instruments:** Q2 revenue was $5.46B and EPS $2.14, ahead of expectations around $5.24B and $1.92. Q3 guidance was also above Street at $5.65B-$6.15B revenue and $2.23-$2.57 EPS, but the stock still slipped after hours.
- **IBM:** Search/live-blog snippets showed the stock rebounding after results around the $205 to $217 area, but the key investor debate remains software durability and AI bookings after earlier revenue concern.
- **ServiceNow:** CNBC reported adjusted EPS of $0.90 on $3.99B revenue, beating estimates, with shares up more than 2% after hours.
- **Las Vegas Sands:** Included in after-hours movers; reaction was weaker in the broad earnings tape, but not central to Adrian's watchlist.
