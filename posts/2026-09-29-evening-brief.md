# Evening Brief — September 29, 2026

*Compiled 8:00 PM CT / 9:00 PM ET*

The single biggest story of the day sits outside the tape entirely: Trump and Speaker Mike Johnson pulled together Elon Musk, Jensen Huang, Sundar Pichai, Dario Amodei, Mark Zuckerberg, and Greg Brockman at the White House to sign **"The White House Accord on Superintelligence: A Joint Commitment on Frontier SI Responsibilities"** — a voluntary industry self-policing pledge. Trump separately floated renaming "AI" to "Super Intelligence," though the accord document itself only uses that phrasing in its title. Layer onto that Anthropic's leaked IPO prospectus (targeting a ~$2 trillion valuation, IPO as soon as November) and OpenAI's confirmation that it's holding back its next flagship model over safety testing, and this was a landmark day for AI policy even as equities themselves had a choppier, quieter session.
