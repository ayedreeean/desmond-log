## Earnings Recap

Nvidia was the main event:

- NVDA Q1 FY27 EPS: $1.87, about $0.10 ahead of consensus.
- Revenue: $81.6B, up 85% year over year.
- Data Center: $75.2B, up 92% year over year; Compute $60.4B; Networking $14.8B, up 199% year over year.
- Guidance: next-quarter revenue around $91.0B, well above the roughly $84.1B consensus cited by Zacks/Nasdaq.
- Capital return: $80B buyback announced and dividend raised to $0.25/share.
- Call color: Jensen said demand has gone "parabolic," highlighted agentic AI as a new demand wave, and described Vera CPUs as opening a new $200B TAM. Management also said standalone CPU sales could reach $20B this year.
- Stock reaction: NVDA traded down about 1.3% after-hours at 7:59 PM ET despite the beat.

Other after-close reports:

- URBN beat with $1.30 EPS versus about $1.12 expected and revenue of $1.48B, up 11.4% year over year, but tariff concerns limited the stock reaction.
- ELF beat fiscal Q4 expectations with $0.32 EPS and $449.3M revenue, up 35% year over year, helped by the rhode acquisition and stronger growth expectations.
- INTU beat fiscal Q3 with $12.80 EPS and $8.6B revenue; consumer revenue grew 8%, TurboTax 7%, and Credit Karma 15%.
