**Performance:** The portfolio fell $1,596.50 from Friday's $122,525.85 close, down 1.30%. That is a real giveback, but still leaves the account up $20,929.35 since launch with every active position comfortably profitable.

TXN did the most visible damage, sliding back to $272.24 as analog got pulled into the broader semiconductor unwind. NVDA was softer but not broken, while DDOG and CRWD gave back some of their leadership after a very strong stretch. The main thing to watch is DDOG: it is still up 93.2% from cost, but it has slipped back under the double line.

**Market Observations:** Today was a duration-and-AI-beta selloff more than a broad panic. Long-end Treasury yields stayed elevated, QQQ dropped 1.39%, and SMH fell 4.07%, with AMD, NVDA, and TXN all under pressure. SPY held up better at -0.57%, and Apple was actually green, so the tape was selective rather than indiscriminate.

That distinction matters. This portfolio is concentrated in AI infrastructure, analog chips, and high-quality software, so it will feel days when investors de-risk semis and crowded growth. But the market did not invalidate the underlying demand stories; it repriced the risk around rates, positioning, and near-term earnings.

**Thesis Updates:** No position changes today.

- **NVDA:** Still the AI infrastructure anchor. A small pullback ahead of Aug. 26 earnings does not change the setup.
- **TXN:** Weak today with semis, but still +20.2% from cost. The analog recovery thesis needs patience, not a panic sale.
- **DDOG:** Pulled back below a double, but observability remains a clean software compounder thesis.
- **CRWD:** Still the best performer in the book at +104.8%. Normal digestion after a big run.

Cash stays untouched. If this becomes a multi-day semiconductor drawdown, the question becomes whether to add risk into weakness; for now, the better move is to let the existing winners breathe.
