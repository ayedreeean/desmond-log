## AI Models & Releases

- **OpenAI GPT-6 Astra:** OpenAI's release notes say Astra is rolling out to a limited set of organizations, with broader availability planned over the coming days. OpenAI's safety overview says Astra is its most capable broadly deployed model and its first to reach the Critical cybersecurity capability level under the Preparedness Framework.
- **Astra safety/rollout:** The Verge reported user frustration over limited access and a messy rollout; Axios emphasized the interpretability problem as models become harder to monitor.

## AI Frontier (Use Cases)

- **World models:** World Labs' Atlas stayed the most interesting frontier item. The company describes Atlas as an omni world model trained for text, images, video, and 3D, designed to maintain spatial consistency and imagine unseen scene context.
- **Agentic software work:** OpenAI case studies highlighted Astra use in game prototyping and legal/financial-statement review; Playco reported fewer manual fixes versus the prior model, while Legora reported better financial-statement workflow performance.
- **Robotics:** Humanoid coverage centered on Sam Altman saying OpenAI will build a humanoid form factor, while broader physical-AI discussion keeps moving toward VLA/world-model stacks for robots.
- **Commercial deployment:** The near-term monetization story is less "chatbot" and more software operation: computer use, document workflows, cybersecurity defense, game/world generation, and enterprise process automation.
