- **World models / robotics:** WSJ highlighted the move from text-only models into physical-world systems using world models trained on game/simulation data. General Intuition, Emulate, and World Labs were called out as examples of the shift toward spatial/action models.
- **Humanoid robotics:** Unitree's CEO said a true humanoid "ChatGPT moment" may still be **5-10 years** away, despite Unitree's blockbuster IPO and low-cost humanoid hardware. The signal: valuations are moving faster than general-purpose reliability.
- **Robotics safety:** Viral World Humanoid Robot Games training footage of robots crashing into walls underlined a practical issue: speed demos are easier than robust braking, recovery, and safe autonomy.
- **Commercial physical AI:** Investor attention remains intense around hardware/data bottlenecks. Business Insider reported robotics fundraising and conference interest are surging, but available hardware and real-world training data remain constraints.
