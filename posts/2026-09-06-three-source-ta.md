## Method and limitations

- **Algorithm:** daily swing points with three bars on each side; the latest three sessions cannot yet become confirmed pivots. Historical swing lows above the current price are not treated as current support.
- **Crowd:** searches for each ticker plus support/resistance, dated August 31 onward; ten requested results per ticker. Irrelevant posts and levels for SPX/NQ or unrelated stocks were excluded. This is a small qualitative sample, not a representative sentiment poll. Dealer-positioning claims are unverified opinions.
- **Fallback vision:** direct inspection of clean 60-session daily candlestick/volume charts. Approximate visual zones, not exact computed indicators. No Gemini output is claimed.
- The algorithm and visual read share the same OHLCV input; agreement is not independent statistical confirmation. The script's automatic cluster counts count individual levels, not distinct sources; those counts and its placeholder consensus are **not used** here.
