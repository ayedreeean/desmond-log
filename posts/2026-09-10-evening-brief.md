## AI Models & Releases

**Confirmed today: DeepSeek V4.1-Flash.** Native text/image input, text output, up to **1M tokens** context. **552B backbone parameters**, with **8B active for input / 16B for output**; the card separately describes **196B conditional-memory parameters**. MIT-licensed weights and inference instructions are available. [Official launch](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/news/news260910/), [model card](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash).

The practical development is a causal encoder–decoder architecture and smaller KV cache, aimed at reducing input-heavy agent costs. Author-reported maximum-effort benchmarks: **Terminal-Bench 2.1 90.6%, DeepSWE 74.2%, GPQA Diamond 90.9%**; newer Terminal-Bench 3.0/4.0 scores are **30.0%/31.2%**. These depend on the specified harness and sampling, and were not independently replicated here. DeepSeek also announced V4-Pro requests will route to V4.1-Flash beginning **September 14, 04:00 UTC**. [Benchmarks](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash), [migration notice](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/news/news260910/).
