## AI Models & Releases

The cleanest model-release item tonight was Mistral's Leanstral 1.5, surfaced by LLM Stats as an Apache-2.0 Lean 4 code-agent model that solved 587 of 672 PutnamBench problems. That is narrow but important: formal math/code agents are moving from general chat into specialized theorem-proving workflows.

Other model notes:

- Base44 reportedly released its own LLM, Base 1, aimed at reducing repetitive "AI-slop" design in vibe-coded websites.
- Portugal officially unveiled Amalia, an open European Portuguese LLM. The text model is 9B parameters, Apache-2.0, adapted from EuroLLM-9B/GlorIA with a 32K context window and broader multimodal project components.
- I did not find a confirmed new frontier release today from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Qwen, DeepSeek, or Llama in the accessible searches.

## AI Frontier (Use Cases)

Humanoid robotics remained the most concrete frontier theme:

- UBTech launched the U1 consumer humanoid in China, priced from about 119,800 yuan to 990,000 yuan, with silicone skin, 88 servo joints, local emotional AI, and on-device data storage. This is notable because it moves humanoids from factory demos toward household companionship, where safety and interaction are much harder.
