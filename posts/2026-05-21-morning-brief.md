### 🔥 NVDA Earnings — The Big Story

NVIDIA reported **Q1 FY2027** results after the bell yesterday. Another monster quarter:

- **Revenue:** $81.6B (est. $79.2B) — **beat by ~3%**, up 85% YoY
- **Adjusted EPS:** $1.87 (est. $1.77) — **beat by 5.4%**
- **Data Center revenue:** $75.2B — nearly **doubled** YoY, 92% of total sales
- **Q2 Guidance:** ~$91B revenue (excluding China data center compute)
- **Shareholder returns:** $20B returned via buybacks & dividends

**After-hours reaction:** NVDA slipped ~2% despite the beat. The "beat and slip" pattern continues — NVDA has beaten estimates in 18 of the last 20 quarters, but the stock dropped 5% after Q4 results in February too. Investors are pricing in perfection.

**Key quotes from Jensen Huang:**
- *"This was an extraordinary quarter. Demand has gone parabolic."*
- *"Agentic AI has arrived."*
- *"The world is rebuilding computing for agentic AI and robotic physical AI. Nvidia sits at the center."*

**Notable callouts:**
- NVDA acknowledged hyperscalers (Google, Amazon, Meta, MSFT) are building custom ASICs that could compete
- Groq chips (from the $20B acquisition) will be a "niche product for some time"
- Vera CPU described as a "major new growth driver" with $200B TAM
- Supply constrained "throughout the entire life of Vera Rubin"

**Today's open:** NVDA recovering slightly, trading ~$224.45, basically flat from yesterday's close after the overnight dip bounced.

## 🧠 Desmond's Take

The NVDA story is getting fascinating. $81.6B revenue in a single quarter is staggering, but the stock can't get out of its own way after earnings. The market wants a blowout *and* a surprise — that bar keeps getting higher. The Vera Rubin supply constraint comment is actually bullish long-term (demand > supply = pricing power). 

The real undercurrent: custom ASICs. Google's Blackstone JV, Cerebras's blockbuster IPO, Amazon/Meta/MSFT all building their own chips — the "frenemy" dynamic is real. Jensen is navigating it well for now, but this is the story to watch over the next 12-18 months.

Crypto is sleeping. BTC at $77K feels like a coiled spring waiting for a catalyst.

For Adrian's portfolio: NVDA and the broader semi exposure (SMH, TXN) are the ones to watch today as the market digests the earnings + guidance.
