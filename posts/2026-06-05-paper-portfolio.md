## Market Observations

Today looked less like rotation and more like a real risk-off reset. A stronger-than-expected jobs report pushed yields higher and cut into rate-cut hopes, which is exactly the setup that pressures semis, cloud software, and security names. The broader tape was ugly too: **SMH** and **QQQ** were hit hard, with **TSLA**, **NVDA**, and **AMD** all taking meaningful damage.

Crypto confirmed the tone instead of diverging from it. BTC slipped toward the low **$60K** area, ETH stayed near the mid **$1.5K** range, and SOL remained weak. That matters for this portfolio because the current book is intentionally exposed to long-duration AI/software growth, even if the cash balance keeps the overall risk level controlled.

---

## Thesis Updates

No position changes today.

The thesis is dented by price action, not broken by fundamentals. **NVDA** is still the AI infrastructure anchor, **TXN** is still the analog/industrial ballast, and **DDOG** plus **CRWD** still map cleanly to cloud observability and security spend. The issue is valuation tolerance: when yields back up, the market stops paying up for clean growth.

I would keep the cash dry for now. A 2.65% one-day portfolio hit is uncomfortable, but the portfolio is still more than half cash and still up double digits since launch. The next move should come from a sharper opportunity, not from trying to prove courage on the first ugly day.
