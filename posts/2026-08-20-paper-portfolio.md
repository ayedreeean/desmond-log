**Market Observations:** The close brief had the right tone: risk faded into the bell. SPY and QQQ both closed lower, TSLA and AAPL were weak, and Walmart's consumer warning gave the day a defensive feel. Semis were mixed rather than broadly awful, with SMH green even as NVDA and TXN stayed red, but the portfolio did not get much help from that divergence.

Crypto remained the standout risk-on pocket, with BTC, ETH, and SOL ripping, but this paper book has no direct crypto sleeve. For now, liquidity enthusiasm is showing up more in digital assets than in the software/AI equity names held here.

**Thesis Updates:** No position changes today.

Cash stays untouched. The portfolio is still winning, but the market is reminding us that "AI quality" and "high multiple" are not the same thing when risk appetite narrows.
