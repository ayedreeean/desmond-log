## Earnings & Data

- **Walmart:** Beat adjusted EPS and revenue, but the stock sold off as U.S. comparable sales slowed to **2.6%** and management sounded cautious about the consumer. AP reported revenue of **$187.94B** and adjusted EPS of **$0.81**.
- **Alibaba:** Profit fell sharply as AI infrastructure spending surged. AP/WSJ reported net profit down about **75%-76%**, revenue up roughly **9%** to nearly **269B yuan**, and AI/cloud investment as the main margin pressure.

- **Geopolitics / oil:** Trump-Iran escalation headlines pushed crude higher and supported energy while weighing on broad risk.
